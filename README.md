## 🚀 What I do

- 🚚 **At Picnic**, I engineer systems that plan how customer orders move through the supply chain, from the warehouse shelf to the customer's door, across European markets.
- 🧮 **Optimization internship:** I spent my summer internship at Picnic on optimization, and I loved it. I ran a feasibility study on replacing a custom algorithm for the Vehicle Routing Problem with Time Windows (VRPTW) with the Timefold solver.
- 🎓 **Education:** I recently finished my BSc in Computer Science at the University of Twente.

## 👨‍🏫 Teaching and mentoring
